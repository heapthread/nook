nook about

nook is a small search page that asks other search engines for you and shows one list. it has no index of its own, no accounts, and no ads.

where results come from

web: {{ engines | join(", ") }}.
images: bing. videos: duckduckgo.
news: {{ outlets }} outlets, grouped by wing in a file you can edit.

what it knows about you

nothing is written down. searches are not logged, and a search is only remembered in memory for a few minutes so a refresh is quick. pictures come through nook, so your browser doesn't visit the sites they came from, and a video doesn't touch youtube until you press play.

things you choose to keep, like the news wing you picked or the bookmarks on the home page, are saved in your own browser and nowhere else.

good to know

results are borrowed, so an engine can be slow or turn nook away for a while. when that happens nook says which one didn't answer. which wing a news outlet sits in is a rough reading of public ratings and is only an opinion.
